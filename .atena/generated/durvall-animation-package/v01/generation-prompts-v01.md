## move

### move_n

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 2D game character sprite-sheet candidate for an isometric action game
Primary request: six distinct sequential running frames for Durvall moving toward the top of the screen, away from the camera; show a clear rear three-quarter view while preserving Durvall's armor and hair identity; the movement direction must read consistently in all six cells.
Input images: Image 1 is the identity and scale anchor (idle); Images 2 and 3 are lateral proportions, armor, weapon, and movement-style references only.
Scene/backdrop: no environment; genuinely transparent outside the character.
Subject: the same full-body Durvall from the references, with pale long hair, pointed ear, dark ornate armor, red waist sash, blue chest mark, and blue-black sword.
Style/medium: preserve the detailed painted game-art rendering and three-quarter isometric camera.
Composition/framing: exactly six equal cells in a 3-column by 2-row grid; one complete character in each cell; fit all hair, sword, limbs, and feet inside each cell; maintain identical body proportions, scale, height, and foot baseline in all six; show six different natural run-cycle poses with changing leg strides, arm motion, torso lean, and hair/sash follow-through.
Lighting/mood: match the references without adding environmental illumination.
Constraints: preserve Durvall's face, anatomy, armor, sword design, palette, and silhouette; keep all empty areas and inter-cell gutters genuinely transparent.
Avoid: background pixels, panels, gradients, floor, ground shadow, colored haze, glow, speed lines, trails, borders, labels, text, duplicate poses, cropped body parts, extra objects, watermark.
```
