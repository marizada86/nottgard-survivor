# Os Pilares — textura-base de platô de obsidiana

Arquivo candidato: `.atena/generated/art-candidates/terrain/pilares-ground-source-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: flat source texture for a modular 2D isometric game terrain atlas; it will be normalized into four seamless 64×32 floor variants for Os Pilares, the endless endgame plateau.
Primary request: strictly flat, low-noise abyssal plateau floor: violet-black obsidian, fractured dark basalt plates, hairline mineral seams in muted smoky lavender, extremely sparse dim astral dust. The surface must feel ancient and terminal while remaining quiet enough to contrast with tall purple abyssal pillars, the final boss, enemies and rotating telegraphs.
Style/medium: crisp pixel art texture, flat top-down material study, no pre-cut diamond tiles.
Color palette: near-black indigo, smoky plum, muted slate violet, tiny desaturated lavender accents; no neon purple.
Constraints: edge-to-edge uniform texture; no raised slabs, props, pillars, runes, glyphs, symbols, portals, water, River Styx, slime, plants, characters, weapons, faces, text, UI, borders, atlas presentation, directional shadows, transparency, blur, 3D render or watermark. Keep detail quiet enough that heroes, enemies, loot, projectiles and all rotating-rule telegraphs stay highly legible at 1280×720.
```

dono e derivada como `assets/tiles/pilares_ground_atlas_v1.png`; a integração
