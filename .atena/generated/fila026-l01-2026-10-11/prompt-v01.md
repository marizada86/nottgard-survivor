Use case: stylized-concept

Input images: Image 1 is a palette/atmosphere reference only (dark abyssal-purple Nottgard title backdrop); do not render its scenery. Image 2 is a material/finish reference only (worn forged old-gold and near-black iron evolution UI frame); do not copy its frame composition or symbols.

Spell each letter correctly: N O T T G A R D and S U R V I V O R S. Top letters are broad Gothic serif capitals, the bottom letters about 45 percent the height, generous tracking. Readability is more important than decoration. Old forged gold with shallow bevel, subtle wear, nearly black outline. A very thin restrained abyssal-purple fissure behind the lettering, contained within the opaque logo contours. Tiny symmetric abstract Gothic arcs and spikes only at the two lateral ends, never over the letters. No other text or symbols.
Style: dense dark-fantasy pixel art, crisp pixels, matches the material family of the supplied UI frame, muted old gold around #D9B866, dark iron, subtle violet #6B2A91 to #B56BFF accents. No glossy plastic 3D, no saturated yellow.
Composition: landscape 3:2 canvas, ideally 1536x1024, with a completely solid uniform cyan #00FFFF backdrop. The logo itself occupies a horizontal band centered vertically, about 1400 pixels wide and 215 to 235 pixels tall, aspect ratio close to 6:1. Plenty of empty cyan above and below, lateral safety margins. Two lines remain separate and easy to read when the cropped logo is reduced to approximately 300x48 pixels. Avoid tall crest, extra central ornament or wide outer frame.
Constraints: every background area AND all holes inside letters and ornaments are exactly flat cyan #00FFFF. Hard edges against the cyan key. No cast shadow, no glow outside contours, no cyan incorporated into the gold/iron object, no background gradient, no characters, no scenery, no readable runes, no heraldic crest, no watermark, no labels, no extra words.
