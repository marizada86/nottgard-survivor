# Goranthis — textura-base de terraço do falso paraíso

Arquivo candidato: `.atena/generated/art-candidates/terrain/goranthis-ground-source-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: flat source texture for a modular 2D isometric game terrain atlas; it will be normalized into four seamless 64×32 floor variants for Goranthis.
Primary request: simplify the terrace floor into a strictly flat, low-noise expanse of broad worn limestone and pale pearl marble. Keep only soft irregular slab seams, hairline fractures, restrained dry olive moss in a few joints, and rare faint honey mineral flecks. Remove all decorative inlay bands, diamond motifs, borders, geometric symbols and ornamental patterns. The surface must be quietly beautiful yet unsettling, highly legible under sanctuary circles, illusion enemies, puddles and waterfall props.
Style/medium: crisp pixel art texture, flat top-down material study, no pre-cut diamond tiles.
Color palette: muted parchment stone, dusty old gold, pearl cream, sparse dry olive; no white glare, no neon.
Constraints: edge-to-edge uniform texture; no raised stones, props, waterfalls, water, River Styx, vegetation, flowers, statues, clouds, characters, faces, symbols, text, UI, borders, atlas presentation, directional shadows, transparency, blur, 3D render or watermark.
```

Origem: geração interna de imagem, 2026-09-27. A candidata foi aprovada
explicitamente pelo dono e derivada como o atlas final
`assets/tiles/goranthis_ground_atlas_v1.png`; a integração foi limitada aos
materiais terrestres dos terraços.
