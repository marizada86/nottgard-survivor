# Shendilavri / Rivenheart — textura-base de mármore ilusório

Arquivo candidato: `.atena/generated/art-candidates/terrain/shendilavri-ground-source-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: flat source texture for a modular 2D isometric game terrain atlas; it will be normalized into four seamless 64×32 floor variants for Shendilavri / Rivenheart.
Primary request: strictly flat, low-noise refined abyssal palace floor: deep violet-black marble slabs, subtle broken joins, hairline muted ruby and silver veins, restrained pale crystal dust caught in seams. The surface must feel luxurious but dangerous, supporting bright pink crystals, succubi, fragile illusion enemies and combat telegraphs without visual competition.
Style/medium: crisp pixel art texture, flat top-down material study, no pre-cut diamond tiles.
Color palette: aubergine black, muted plum, smoked burgundy, faint cold silver, very sparse desaturated crystal-pink flecks; no neon.
Constraints: edge-to-edge uniform texture; no raised slabs, props, crystals, mirrors, reflections that resemble characters, torii, shrines, statues, furniture, lanterns, water, River Styx, slime, plants, symbols, text, UI, borders, atlas presentation, directional shadows, transparency, blur, 3D render or watermark. Keep the surface detail quiet enough that heroes, enemies, loot, projectiles and telegraphs stay highly legible at 1280×720.
```
