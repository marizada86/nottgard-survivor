# Feng-tu — textura-base de pátio ritual

Arquivo candidato: `.atena/generated/art-candidates/terrain/feng-tu-ground-source-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: flat source texture for a modular 2D isometric game terrain atlas; it will be normalized into four seamless 64×32 floor variants for Feng-tu.
Primary request: strictly flat, low-noise ancient temple courtyard floor: blue-black slate and volcanic basalt paving, subtly offset broad stone seams, hairline cracks, a small amount of matte gray ritual ash caught between joints, and extremely rare dull copper mineral flecks. The surface must read as a restrained sacred court under red torii props and lightning telegraphs.
Style/medium: crisp pixel art texture, flat top-down material study, no pre-cut diamond tiles.
Color palette: charcoal blue-black, cool slate gray, muted ash gray, tiny tarnished copper accents; no bright red, no neon.
Constraints: edge-to-edge uniform texture; no raised stones, props, torii gates, shrines, statues, lanterns, characters, weapons, corpses, water, River Styx, slime, plants, symbols, text, UI, borders, atlas presentation, directional shadows, transparency, blur, 3D render, or watermark. Keep surface detail quiet enough that heroes, enemies, loot, projectiles and lightning telegraphs stay highly legible at 1280×720.
```

Origem: geração interna de imagem, 2026-09-27. A candidata foi aprovada
explicitamente pelo dono e derivada como o atlas final
`assets/tiles/feng_tu_ground_atlas_v1.png`; a integração foi limitada aos
materiais terrestres do pátio ritual.
