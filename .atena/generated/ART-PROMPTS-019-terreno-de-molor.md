# Molor — textura-base de caverna de detrito

Arquivo candidato: `.atena/generated/art-candidates/terrain/molor-ground-source-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: flat source texture for a modular 2D isometric game terrain atlas; it will be normalized into four seamless 64×32 floor variants for Molor.
Primary request: strictly flat, low-noise cavern floor texture: compact charcoal-green bedrock, subtle crushed mineral detritus, hairline cracks, tiny embedded grit, and rare dull olive residue. Molor should feel like a decomposed cesspool, but the floor must be quiet and broadly navigable.
Style/medium: crisp pixel art texture, flat top-down material study, no pre-cut diamond tiles.
Color palette: deep charcoal green-black, muted bone-brown, oxidized olive; no neon.
Constraints: edge-to-edge uniform texture; no raised rocks, props, pipes, bones, skulls, shells, bubbles, characters, architecture, water, River Styx, symbols, text, UI, borders, atlas presentation, directional shadows, transparency, blur, 3D render or watermark. Keep texture scale fine enough that heroes, enemies, loot, projectiles and telegraphs stay legible at 1280×720.
```

Origem: geração interna de imagem, 2026-09-27. A candidata foi aprovada
explicitamente pelo dono e derivada como o atlas final
`assets/tiles/molor_ground_atlas_v1.png`; a integração foi limitada aos
materiais de piso de Molor.
